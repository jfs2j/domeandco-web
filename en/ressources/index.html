Frequently asked

Quick answers before we speak

When does a food business need an HACCP plan?

From the moment you start selling food to consumers or trade buyers. Under French and European regulations, every food operator must demonstrate control over hygiene hazards, even in a small workshop.

What happens during the free 30-minute consultation?

We speak by video call or phone about your product, current workshop setup and regulatory or commercial hurdles, and pinpoint the first two or three priorities.

Do you only work in Greater Paris?

DŌMĘ & Co is based in Greater Paris, yet we travel on-site across France and support clients remotely across Europe and within the African diaspora.
