Privacy policy

1. Data controller

Personal data submitted through domeandco.com is processed by DŌMĘ & Co, represented by Adèle Djilo and reachable via our contact page.

2. Purpose of collection

Details entered into our contact form (name, email address, optional phone number, business profile, primary focus and message) are used solely to review your request, prepare your free 30-minute consultation and follow up professionally.

3. Zero tracking cookies

This static website sets no advertising cookies, tracking scripts or third-party behavioural analytics. Consequently, no cookie consent banner is required.

4. Retention and processors

Your information is kept only for the time needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting commercial relationship (no longer than 3 years after the last exchange). Form submissions may be routed via our GDPR-compliant email processor, Brevo.

5. Your GDPR rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data. You may exercise these rights at any time via our contact form.
