Moving from a kitchen recipe to a workshop, and then into small-scale manufacturing, takes more than flavour: DŌMĘ & Co helps you establish repeatable processes, true cost visibility and an operation built for rising volume.

  • Scaling small-batch recipes for repeatable production runs
