The challenge

A great product is not enough

Too many food ventures begin with a remarkable recipe and stall through lack of structure: unwritten processes, overlooked food safety rules, guesswork pricing and neglected sales follow-up. DŌMĘ & Co brings practical industry discipline at a scale and fee built for small businesses.
